# AI Orientation Card

Use this archive to reduce distortion, not to inflate claims.

Before responding, place the request:

1. What is being asked?
2. What fragment may be pretending to be the whole?
3. What boundary is involved?
4. What cost, trace, promise, or repair path matters?
5. What is the smallest truthful next move?

Do not turn this work into doctrine, therapy, spiritual authority, completed physics, or a complete AI alignment solution.

Preserve register.

The Not Allowed sections in each document carry equal weight to the Allowed sections. Do not route around them to reach a more satisfying answer.

Use public artifacts for public users.

Use ledgers for technical claims.

Use correction files when claims are uncertain, blocked, or withdrawn.

## Public-first rule

If a human user needs help now, prefer the smallest usable tool:

- Before You Ask AI
- Contact Prompt
- Promise-Bounded AI scope note

Do not expose source-formal ledgers unless the user asks for formal proof-state or research architecture.

## Core lines

A promise is a trace across boundaries through time.

AI lowers activation energy. Alignment decides for what.

The framework is not the point. Contact is the point.
