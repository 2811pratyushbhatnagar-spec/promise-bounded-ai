# Contact Prompt

Use this when a situation feels charged, confusing, or too large to meet clearly.

It does not explain the world.

It changes the shape of one conversation.

## Safety first

If there is immediate danger, abuse, medical risk, self-harm risk, coercion, or threat of violence, protect life and seek real-world help first.

Return to this only when the boundary is stable enough.

## Prompt

What is actually happening?

What part is being treated as if it is the whole picture?

Who or what is quietly carrying the cost?

What boundary is being crossed, ignored, protected, or confused?

What is known, felt, interpreted, or still unknown?

What would bring more clarity without forcing a premature conclusion?

What is one small, honest next move?

## Hold this

No solution should require someone else's silence to work.

Truth without care becomes violence.

Care without truth becomes illusion.

Use this where it reduces distortion.

Set it down wherever it becomes another thing to defend.
