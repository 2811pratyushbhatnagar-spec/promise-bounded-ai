# Promise-Bounded AI

Small tools for making clearer AI requests, better promises, and more repairable actions.

This is not a belief system.
Use it where it reduces distortion.
Set it down wherever it becomes another thing to defend.

## What are you trying to do?

- I am about to ask AI for something → [Before You Ask AI](before-you-ask-ai.md)
- I am designing an AI action → [Promise-Bounded AI](promise-bounded-ai.md)
- I am in a difficult situation → [Contact Prompt](contact-prompt.md)
- I want the research archive → [Research](research.md)

## Boundary

The public site should make contact easier.
The archive should make claims inspectable.
Do not make either one impersonate the other.
